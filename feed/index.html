About Our Medical Centre in Wodonga

Elmwood Medical Centre has been providing trusted healthcare to the Wodonga community since 2007. As a locally doctor-owned GP clinic in Wodonga, our experienced team is committed to delivering high-quality, patient-centred care for individuals and families at every stage of life.

As a comprehensive medical centre in Wodonga, Elmwood Medical Centre offers a wide range of general practice and primary healthcare services in one convenient location. Whether you are looking for a GP in Wodonga, a local doctor in Wodonga, or ongoing support for your health, our team provides professional medical care in a welcoming and caring environment.

Our goal is to support better health outcomes for our patients through accessible, comprehensive and personalised healthcare. We are proud to be part of the Wodonga community and to continue caring for local patients and families.

Our Values

  • Provide patient-centred care, taking into account the psychosocial, spiritual and physical needs of each individual.
  • Strive to achieve best practice standards and continuous improvement in all aspects of patient care.
  • To maintain professional standards of confidentiality of all information pertaining to patients.
  • Foster a culture of respect, support, education, teamwork and open communication between patients and all involved at Elmwood Medical Centre

If you or your family needs a doctor on a weeknight, weekend or public holiday for urgent medical care please use  National Home Doctor service. Always call 000 in an emergency.

Elmwood Medical Centre is a mixed-billed clinic. Bulk-billed appointments are available with selected doctors only and will be clearly displayed next to the doctor’s name when booking through HotDoc. All over 70 Years are bulk billed for standard consultation, and those who have paid the first GAP. Additional fees apply for services such as wound dressings, ECGs, skin checks, procedures, and other non-standard consultations. Please speak with our reception team before your appointment if you would like further information about applicable fees.
