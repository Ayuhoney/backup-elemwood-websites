Pre-Employment Medicals in Wodonga

We provide comprehensive pre-employment medical assessments to help employers ensure staff are fit for work.

Our assessments help:

  • Identify pre-existing conditions
  • Reduce workplace risk and injury
  • Support workplace safety compliance
  • Minimise future WorkCover claims

Truck, Taxi & Commercial Driver Medicals

We offer medical assessments for:

  • Truck licence medicals
  • Taxi licence medicals
  • Commercial driver medicals

Our doctors assess your fitness to safely operate a vehicle in line with relevant medical standards.

Fast turnaround times available to help you stay on the road and compliant.

What’s Included in Our Medical Assessments

Our employment and occupational health assessments may include:

  • Drug and alcohol screening
  • Audiometry (hearing tests)
  • Spirometry (lung function testing)
  • Vision testing
  • Blood pressure checks
  • Body mass index (BMI)
  • Urine analysis
  • Functional and strength testing
  • Joint range of motion
  • Electrocardiogram (ECG)

Occupational Health Services Wodonga

Elmwood Medical Centre provides ongoing occupational health services for businesses and workers across Wodonga.

✔ Pre-employment assessments
✔ Workplace medicals
✔ Injury management
✔ Return-to-work planning

We work closely with employers to support a safe, healthy workforce.

WorkCover & Injury Management

Our team provides full WorkCover support, including:

  • Initial injury assessment
  • Ongoing treatment and monitoring
  • Return-to-work planning
  • Specialist and allied health referrals

Our goal is to help patients recover and safely return to work.

Elmwood Medical Centre is a mixed-billed clinic. Bulk-billed appointments are available with selected doctors only and will be clearly displayed next to the doctor’s name when booking through HotDoc. All over 70 Years are bulk billed for standard consultation, and those who have paid the first GAP. Please speak with our reception team before your appointment if you would like further information about applicable fees.
